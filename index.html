1. The World

1.1

Nvidia CEO Huang resigns amid Chinese spy affair

Jensen Huang resigned November 12 as Nvidia CEO and board member following reports of affair with suspected Chinese intelligence agent. CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO. Three House committees summoned Huang for December 1 testimony. Investigators probing whether linked company smuggled billions in restricted chips to China.

Confidence: multiple independent sources
1.2

Microsoft outage disrupts 74 countries for 12 hours

Microsoft services including Outlook, Teams and SharePoint knocked out globally starting 16:00 GMT November 12, affecting businesses and schools in 74 countries. Nearly 130,000 US outage reports logged. Microsoft reported only marginal recovery after 12 hours, ruled out malicious activity. Service remained partially disrupted November 13.

Sources: ABC News (center, T1); NBC News (center, T1); Washington Post (left, T1); Al Jazeera (center, T1)
Confidence: multiple independent sources
1.3

Ukraine and Russia agree POW exchange exceeding 1,000

Russia and Ukraine agreed November 12 in Abu Dhabi to exchange over 1,000 prisoners of war from each side within two to three weeks, largest humanitarian release since 2022. US envoys Kushner and Witkoff mediated. Talks yielded no ceasefire or territorial breakthrough; Putin reiterated demands for Ukrainian oblasts.

2. By Region

2.1 [Middle East]

Tankers catch fire in Hormuz as Iran maintains closure

Three oil tankers exploded and caught fire in Strait of Hormuz during coverage window. IRGC reported tankers struck mines in illegal passageways. Iran Security Chief Mohsen Rezaei said November 12 Tehran still awaits US answer to seven peace conditions, strait remains closed until met. Hormuz normally carries one-fifth global oil, severely restricted since February 28.

Sources: Al Arabiya (center, T1); Tehran Times (center, T2); Jerusalem Post (right, T2); Al Jazeera (center, T1)
Confidence: multiple independent sources
2.2 [Africa]

DRC Ebola outbreak reaches 10,990 cases

As of November 9, DRC reported 10,990 confirmed Ebola cases including 5,354 deaths across eight provinces, second-largest outbreak in history. Bundibugyo virus strain identified. WHO Director-General briefed member states November 11. Armed groups killing more people than Ebola itself, attacking clinics. Three clinics torched, hundreds of patients scattered.

Sources: WHO (center, T1); ECDC (center, T1); UN News (center, T1); Al Jazeera (center, T1)
Confidence: multiple independent sources
2.3 [Asia-Pacific]

Indonesia VP resigns over credentials dispute

Gibran Rakabuming Raka, son of former President Jokowi, submitted resignation November 12 after Constitutional Court and Supreme Court ruled his overseas education documents invalid. Lawmakers pushed impeachment over inadequate credentials. Upper house must declare vacancy and request two replacement candidates from President Prabowo.

3. Energy

3.1

Saudi oil output surges 26 percent to wartime high

Saudi Arabia reported October crude production reached 10.56 million barrels per day, up 26 percent from September's 8.37 million, marking highest output since February when US-Iran conflict began. Independent secondary sources estimated lower 9.19 million bpd. Recovery reflects improved Strait of Hormuz shuttle operations and Red Sea pipeline use.

Sources: Bloomberg (center, T1); Al Jazeera (center, T1); Oilprice.com (center, T2); Arab News (center, T2)
Confidence: multiple independent sources
3.2

IEA forecasts oil surplus despite diesel squeeze

IEA Oil Market Report projects global oil market returning to surplus in Q4 2026, expanding to 3.8 million bpd surplus in 2027 as Gulf exports recover. However, diesel remains critically tight with European prices near $180 per barrel, 77 percent above pre-war levels. IEA releasing emergency diesel stocks to bridge supply gap.

Sources: IEA Oil Market Report November 2026 (primary, T1); Business Recorder (center, T2); Xinhua (center, T2)
Confidence: single source
3.3

European gas storage hits multi-year low entering winter

EU gas storage stands at 71.6 percent full as of November 10, down 10.8 points year-on-year and 17.5 points below five-year average, lowest for this date since 2017. Dutch TTF benchmark rose 4.2 percent to EUR 96.18 per MWh on November 13, posting 12 percent weekly gain. Commission lowered 2026 storage target to 75-80 percent.

Sources: Voltstack (center, T2); Energy Risk IQ (center, T2); Azernews (center, T2)
Confidence: multiple independent sources

4. Economy

4.1

US wholesale inflation hits four-year high, Fed hike certain

Producer prices rose 6.5 percent year-over-year in October, highest since November 2022. Monthly gain of 0.3 percent, matching Fed Governor Waller's threshold for considering 50-basis-point December hike. CME FedWatch shows 86 percent odds of 25bp hike, 14 percent for 50bp. Core PPI up 5 percent annually.

Sources: CNN (center, T1); Business Standard (center, T2); Investing.com (center, T2); CME Group FedWatch (primary)
Confidence: multiple independent sources
4.2

Ten-year Treasury yield reaches 5.16 percent

Benchmark US 10-year yield hit 5.16 percent on Friday November 13, highest since October, tracking hawkish Fed comments and hot inflation data. 30-year bond auction Thursday cleared at 5.441 percent yield. German 10-year Bund climbed to 3.357 percent, two-week high. Global bond selloff ended month-long sovereign debt rally.

Sources: CNBC (center, T1); Bloomberg (center, T1); Yahoo Finance (center, T2); Trading Economics (primary)
Confidence: multiple independent sources
4.3

Fed officials converge on hawkish stance

Governor Waller advocates 50bp hike if core CPI hits 0.3 percent monthly. Dallas Fed's Logan wants two more 25bp increases. New York Fed's Williams says more rate increases likely with persistent inflation. Cleveland Fed's Hammack warns inflation moving in wrong direction. Unified hawkish messaging signals prolonged restrictive policy.

Sources: Reuters via Investing.com (center, T1); Yahoo Finance (center, T2); Federal Reserve statements (primary); Washington Times (right, T2)
Confidence: multiple independent sources

President Prabowo must nominate two candidates within 14 days; parliament decides within 60 days. Highest-profile resignation since Suharto 1998.

US envoys meet Putin after Ukraine talks

Presidential envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff met Vladimir Putin November 12 following two-day Abu Dhabi negotiations between Russia and Ukraine. Putin told envoys European troops in Ukraine post-ceasefire absolutely unacceptable and would be legitimate targets. Talks yielded prisoner swap agreement but no broader breakthrough.

Iran claims Bahrain radar strike, US denies

Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps claimed November 13 it destroyed US early-warning radar system in Bahrain. US Central Command denied claim, saying two Iranian attack drones fired overnight were shot down with no injuries or facility damage. Neither Bahraini government nor military verified any strike.
