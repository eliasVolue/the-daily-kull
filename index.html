The timetable correction, and it is this paper's own error. Dagens Nyheter reported a mandatory confidence vote on the sitting prime minister that this paper's standing Riksdag timetable did not carry. The standing note was wrong by omission and the report was right. Chapter 6 Article 3 requires a newly elected Riksdag to test by vote whether the prime minister has sufficient support within two weeks of assembling, so with the roll call on 28 September the outer limit is 12 October. The threshold is negative parliamentarism: Kristersson falls only if more than half of all members, 175 of 349, actively vote no, so abstention sustains him and the 176 to 173 bloc arithmetic is exactly the margin to watch. The vote is skipped entirely if he has already resigned or been dismissed, which is the likeliest reason it never entered these notes. It sits beside the formation sequence rather than inside it: Article 3 tests the sitting government, Article 4 governs formation, and under Article 4 the Speaker must confer with every party group and the deputy speakers before submitting a proposal, which the Riksdag then decides within four days without committee preparation. So the real clock is 28 September roll call and Speaker election, 29 September session opening, the Article 3 vote at any point up to 12 October, and formation beginning only once Kristersson goes. This is the second time in three issues that a standing correction in this paper's own memory was itself the stale thing.
