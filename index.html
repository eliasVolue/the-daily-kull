Top line

Ukraine dropped a Kerch Bridge span into the sea and Iran struck Gulf shipping and Bahrain again, two days before Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen.

1 · The World

1 · Ukraine drops a span of the Kerch Bridge into the sea

Overnight into 17 November Ukrainian missiles and drones brought down a road span of the Kerch Bridge, Russia's main road link to Crimea. Road traffic is halted and debris lies in the Azov shipping channel.

Ukrinform citing the General Staff (center, T2); The Guardian (left, T2); NBC News (left, T2); NPR (left, T2); Maritime Executive (center, T2); Reuters (center, T2); The Telegraph and The Times (right, T2, headlines). Confidence: multiple independent sources. Casualty and drone-count claims left out as unchecked. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · Iran hits Gulf shipping and Bahrain again

The LPG carrier Tara Gas, first struck off Khasab on Monday evening, was hit again on 17 November; its crew abandoned ship, one sailor missing. Iranian drones targeted Bahrain for a third time in days; Bahrain says it intercepted them.

gCaptain citing UKMTO warnings 207-26 and 208-26 (center, T3); Reuters (center, T2); Al Jazeera (center, T2); CNBC (center, T2); Fox News (right, T2); Anadolu (center, T2). Attacker on Tara Gas unidentified; the US rejects Iran's claim to have hit a radar. Confidence: multiple independent sources. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · CNN: US intelligence sees Iranian attack options in Washington

CNN reported late on 16 November that US intelligence shows Iran developing options to strike senior officials in Washington with a dirty bomb or chemical weapon, nothing imminent. Trump warned Iran would pay the price; Tehran dismissed it.

CNN (left, T2, 16 Nov 22:59 GMT); CNBC (center, T2); Washington Examiner (right, T2); The Washington Post (left, T2); DW (center, T2, Iran denial). Every outlet rests on CNN's single intelligence report; the White House said there were no credible threats to the homeland. Verification: single source, unconfirmed.

2 · By region

Middle East · Saudi-backed forces dig in as the Houthis close on Aden

Saudi-backed Yemeni forces fortified lines outside Aden on 17 November, with the Houthis within about 100 km, Reuters reported. Maersk and Hapag-Lloyd, phasing a return to Suez routings by mid-January, face renewed Red Sea risk.

Reuters via US News (center, T2); Agenzia Nova (center, T2); NBC News (left, T2); The Loadstar (center, T2, Suez return); Jerusalem Post (right, T2). Distance figures vary by outlet. Confidence: multiple independent sources. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

North America · Carney and Xi widen the Canada-China tariff truce

In Shenzhen on 17 November, Carney won Chinese tariff cuts on about 400 million Canadian dollars of pork and seafood; Canada extended its steel and aluminium tariff relief for Chinese imports for two years.

Reuters, Bloomberg, The Globe and Mail, SCMP and Anadolu headlines (center, T2); The Western Producer (center, T2); National Observer (left, T2). Full texts were not reachable to the checker. Confidence: multiple independent sources. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

Middle East · Britain sanctions eight Israeli settlement builders

On 17 November Britain froze assets of eight Israeli settlement builders and of Tzav 9, which blocks Gaza aid, ahead of final E1 tender deadlines; E1 would cut the West Bank off from East Jerusalem.

GOV.UK release (T1, read directly); Reuters via US News (center, T2); AP via WTOP (center, T2); Israel Hayom (right, T2). Directors of the nine entities are also disqualified. Verification: verified, primary source.

3 · Energy

1 · Four of Sweden's six reactors are offline; prices ease

Forsmark 3 tripped on 14 November; with Forsmark 1 and Ringhals 3 and 4 out, only Forsmark 2 and Oskarshamn 3 run. Wednesday's day-ahead eased to EUR 151.68 per MWh in SE3 and 178.54 in SE4, from 205.10 and 227.14.

Vattenfall notice, 14 Nov (T1); SVT and Sveriges Radio (center, T2); energy-charts.info, all 96 quarter-hours per zone recomputed independently by two desks for 18 November delivery (T1). Germany 146.25, SE2 58.64. Restarts forecast 20 to 27 November (hurbra.se, T3, dates change often). Verification: verified, primary source.

2 · Oil settles lower as the surplus outweighs the attacks

Brent settled at $92.44 on Monday, down 3.5 percent, as the IEA's projected fourth-quarter surplus outweighed the tanker attacks, then rose about 1 percent on Tuesday. TTF held near EUR 85 to 86 per MWh on Tuesday.

Xinhua settlement wire via The Star (center, T2); Iraqi News Agency (center, T2); Markets Insider (center, T2); CNBC citing Reuters (center, T2); OilPrice.com (center, T3, TTF). Tuesday figures are intraday, not settlements; EU storage left out as unverifiable this issue. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Arab states and Russia reject COP31's fossil fuel text

Saudi-led Arab states and Russia on 17 November rejected a COP31 draft cover text calling for an “immediate” transition away from fossil fuels and a 35 percent electricity share of final energy by 2035. Talks end 20 November.

Climate Home News, Day 8 bulletin (center, T2); Politico Europe (center, T2, draft published 16 Nov before the cutoff); Reuters (center, T2); Down to Earth (center, T2). Only the word “immediate” is confirmed verbatim. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

4 · Economy

1 · Fed's Musalem says several hikes are likely

St. Louis Fed President Alberto Musalem said on 17 November the economy will likely need “several” rate increases before spring 2027, from 3.75 to 4.00 percent. October import prices rose 1.1 percent, above forecasts; the 10-year yield is 5.12.

CNBC (center, T2, read directly); Reuters, Bloomberg and The Wall Street Journal headlines (center and right, T2); US Treasury par yield curve, 16 Nov (T1). CNBC's reading of up to four hikes by April is the reporter's inference, not Musalem's words. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · AI stocks slide before Nvidia results as China threatens retaliation

The Nasdaq fell 2.5 percent and Nvidia 4.1 percent on 16 November; Nvidia reports after Tuesday's close. Beijing called the US chip licence rule “economic coercion” and vowed “necessary measures”; its suspended gallium export ban lapses 27 November.

FRED Nasdaq Composite, recomputed (T1); AP (center, T2); CNN (left, T2); Global Times (state, T2); Macau Business (center, T2); Fox News (right, T2); Reuters (center, T2, the one-year suspension, November 2025). Confidence: multiple independent sources. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · German investor expectations hit a nine-month high

ZEW's German expectations index rose to 35.6 in November from 28.1, against 28.8 expected, the highest since February; the euro area reading rose to 26.5. ZEW credits rising orders and cheaper oil but flags public debt sustainability.

ZEW press release (T1, read directly); investingLive (center, T2, consensus); Anadolu Agency (center, T2, “highest since February”, not in the release). Verification: verified, primary source.

5 · Politics

1 · Sweden's Speaker pauses government talks for a week

Speaker Norlén on 17 November granted Kristersson a one-week pause, with a third prime minister vote possible by month end. Two of four votes are spent; a fourth failure forces an extra election.

SVT (center, T2); Sveriges Radio (center, T2); Dagens Nyheter (left, T2); Svenska Dagbladet (right, T2); Aftonbladet (left, T2). How it works: a candidate passes unless 175 of 349 members vote against; Norlén wants it settled well before Christmas, and the next vote need not be on Kristersson. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · Europe joins the Ukraine talks; Moscow rules out NATO entry

Five European governments joined US-Ukraine talks online for the first time on 16 November; the Kremlin said it never agreed to that format. On 17 November Moscow ruled out Ukraine joining NATO.

Al Jazeera (center, T2); Kyiv Post (center, T2); Reuters via Jerusalem Post citing Interfax (center, T2, Ryabkov); RTE (center, T2): Zelensky says US envoys had floated NATO membership in exchange for Donbas. The five: France, Germany, Britain, Italy and Poland. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Burnham keeps EU red lines until the next election

Prime Minister Burnham said on 16 November he would not cross his EU red lines before the next election, leaving deeper ties to the next Parliament. Friday's summit should link UK and EU carbon markets.

GOV.UK speech text, Lord Mayor's Banquet (T1, read directly); Politico Europe (center, T2); Bloomberg via ThePrint and PA via Nation.Cymru (center, T2, carbon market link, not in the speech). Verification: verified, reliable secondary.
