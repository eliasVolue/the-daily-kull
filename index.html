Top line

Sweden's centre-left leads by three seats with the final result due next weekend, Saudi Arabia shut its Hormuz bypass pipeline after drone strikes, and Brent traded above $108.

1 · The World

1 · Saudi Arabia shuts its Hormuz bypass pipeline

Drones struck pumping stations in the Riyadh and Madinah regions overnight on 10 and 11 September, and Riyadh shut the East-West line on 11 September as a precaution. It had been carrying 4 to 5 million barrels a day. Brent traded as high as $108.65 on Monday against Friday's $104.61 close.

Saudi Ministry of Energy statement via wires (primary, T1); Al Jazeera (center-left, T2); CNBC (center, T1); Bloomberg (center, T1); CNN (left, T1); Washington Times (right, T2); Argus Media (center, T2); Investing.com for the price (center, T3). Verification: verified, reliable secondary. The shutdown is confirmed, which upgrades Friday's unconfirmed satellite thermal signatures. A capacity of 7 million barrels a day was cut: it traces to one trade-press item, while every T1 carrier and the US Energy Information Administration give 4 to 5 million, so only the throughput figure ships. The attack is dated across both nights because Al Jazeera puts it on 11 September and the desk's original read on the 10th. No group has claimed it; Saudi Arabia and the Iraqi Prime Minister's office attribute the drones to Iraqi territory on the record, and the “ordered from Tehran” line in circulation is a named analyst's speculation, so it is not carried.

2 · Oman's Hormuz talks are postponed as Houthis take Mayun

Oman postponed Monday's Salalah meeting between Gulf foreign ministers and Iran “in the interests of consensus”, its foreign minister said. A cargo ship was struck off Qeshm early Sunday, one of ten crew killed. Houthi forces seized Mayun on 11 September, the island dividing Bab el-Mandeb into its two shipping lanes.

UKMTO advisory (primary, T1); Bloomberg (center, T1); Reuters via Investing.com (center, T1); ABC News (center, T1); Euronews (center, T2); Al Jazeera (center-left, T2); NPR and NBC News (left, T1); Washington Times (right, T2). Verification: verified, reliable secondary. A desk draft calling the talks a collapse was corrected to postponed: every source says postponed, and Iranian state media describe a joint Tehran and Muscat decision taken at the request of regional states. Mayun was described as the only inhabited island inside the strait; no source carries that, so the sourced description of what it controls is used instead. Nobody has claimed the Qeshm strike, which UKMTO calls an unknown projectile, so the item attributes it to no one.

3 · US inflation holds at 3.4 percent with the Fed deciding Wednesday

August consumer prices rose 0.4 percent on the month and 3.4 percent over twelve months, unchanged from July. Core was 0.3 percent monthly and 2.4 percent annually, and the energy index rose 16.3 percent over the year. A quarter-point rise on Wednesday would be the first increase of this cycle.

Bureau of Labor Statistics consumer price release, 11 September, read directly (primary, T1); CNBC (center, T1); Wall Street Journal (right, T1); CBS News (center-left, T1). Verification: verified, primary source. All five figures match the release exactly. Friday's prediction-market odds were cut from this item entirely and rebuilt in the Economy section, because they moved over the weekend.

2 · By region

Europe · A Russian drone hit a Warsaw-bound train two kilometres from Poland

A drone struck the locomotive of a Kyiv to Warsaw train carrying 208 people at Yahodyn, two kilometres inside Ukraine. There were no casualties. Polish authorities identified the type as a Geran-5. Areas near the Ukrainian border went under an air-raid alert.

Euromaidan Press (center, T3); NPR (left, T1); KPBS (center, T2); Polish State Railways passenger figure via the wire coverage (primary via carrier, T2). Verification: verified, reliable secondary. Four desk claims were repaired before publication. The passenger count is 208 people, not 206. A fifteen-minute warning appears in no source; what is reported is that a monitoring team detected the threat and warned the crew in advance. Polish jets scrambling and a Tusk security meeting could not be found in any source and were cut, leaving the air-raid alert that is reported. The Geran-5 identification is attributed to Polish authorities rather than stated as fact.

Asia and Pacific · BRICS names the EU carbon border levy as protectionism

The 140-point New Delhi Declaration, adopted on 12 September on the summit's first day with Putin, Xi and Pezeshkian present, condemned unilateral tariffs and carbon border measures imposed under environmental pretexts. On energy it called for protecting critical infrastructure and undisrupted flows, naming no country.

Prime Minister of India, declaration text (primary, T1); All India Radio news, newsonair.gov.in (primary, T1); Forbes India (center, T2); Business Standard (center, T2); Outlook India (center, T2); Al Jazeera (center-left, T1). Verification: verified, primary source. The adoption date was contested between desks at 12 and 13 September; the Indian state broadcaster's own headline settles it at the first day, 12 September. Two clauses were cut as unsupported by the text: a “maximum restraint” line about Hormuz, and a statement that the declaration ruled out a common currency, where the sourced language is that there is no one-size-fits-all approach.

North America · Carney explores an EU associate status, and Ottawa pushes back

The Wall Street Journal reported on Sunday that Carney is exploring an unprecedented European Union associate member status covering goods, services and workers in strategic supply chains such as energy, defence and critical minerals. An unnamed Canadian government source told CTV the report overstates it and that Canada seeks no membership in any capacity.

Wall Street Journal, originating report (right, T1); CTV News (center, T2); Euronews (center, T2); UPI (center, T2); The Japan Times (center, T2). Verification: single source, unconfirmed. One scoop resting on unnamed officials on both sides, with every other outlet a carrier, so the label is mandatory. The report landed Sunday, not Saturday as a desk had it. A desk claim of visa-free rights to live and work was cut as overstated. The Canadian denial arrived the same day and was missing from the draft; it is now in the item. Carney addresses the European Parliament on Thursday.

3 · Energy

1 · The IEA cuts oil demand again as diesel sets another US record

The Oil Market Report of 11 September cut 2026 demand a further 940,000 barrels a day, to a 2.5 million decline. Supply falls 5.7 million to 100.7 million, with stocks drawn 507 million barrels since February. US pump diesel set a nominal record of $6.2301 a gallon on Monday, from $5.9015 a week earlier.

International Energy Agency, Oil Market Report September 2026, title confirmed on the agency's own listing and the report read directly (primary, T1); AAA Gas Prices, read directly (primary, T1); Euronews (center, T2). Verification: verified, primary source. Every number matches the primary, and the diesel series is internally consistent with Friday's $6.0556 sitting between the week-ago and today's prints. A headline claiming the agency had declared refining the binding constraint was cut: the report's own finding is that losses concentrate in middle distillates and petrochemical feedstocks, which is a narrower claim.

2 · Dutch gas reaches €84, the highest in more than three years

TTF front month peaked at €83.94 a megawatt hour on Monday and stood near €83.39 at press time, up about 4.9 percent on the day and 35 percent on the month, on the Saudi closure and curtailed Qatari cargoes. European storage was about 68 percent full on the 13 September gas day.

Trading Economics (center, T3); Bloomberg (center, T1); Euronews (center, T2); Gas Infrastructure Europe AGSI+ via EnergyRiskIQ for storage (primary via carrier, T3). Verification: single source, unconfirmed on the storage figure; the price is verified, reliable secondary. A desk gave the intraday peak as the day's level, an error this desk has caught before, so the peak and the press-time level are now both printed. The monthly change is rounded down to 35 percent. “Highest in more than three years” holds: December 2022 is three years and nine months back. A European Commission line on winter security was cut as unverified.

Monday day-ahead averaged €270.11 a megawatt hour in SE4 and €144.81 in SE3, against €99.64 in SE3 on Friday. SE4's quarter-hour peak was €797.48 at 19:30. Germany averaged €256.55 and never fell below €152.00 all day. Swedish nuclear availability is 3,632 of 7,054 megawatts.

energy-charts.info reading Nord Pool, ENTSO-E, Bundesnetzagentur and SMARD, all 96 quarter-hourly values fetched and averaged independently by both the data desk and the Fact-checker (primary, T1); Nordic Nuclear Power reactor register (T3). The Federal Reserve decides Wednesday, the Bank of England Thursday and the Bank of Japan Friday. Polymarket prices a quarter-point Fed rise at 79.5 percent this morning and Kalshi at 78.5, both up from below 60 on Friday, closing most of the gap with futures near 85 percent.

2 · The thirty-year Treasury holds its highest level since 2007

Federal Reserve H.15 and the Treasury par yield curve put the ten-year at 4.95 percent and the thirty-year at 5.37 on 10 September, the thirty-year the highest since 2007. On Friday the ten-year rose to 4.96 and the thirty-year eased to 5.35. Thirty-year gilts are near 5.9.

US Treasury daily par yield curve file and Federal Reserve H.15, both read directly (primary, T1); Trading Economics for the gilt (center, T3); wire coverage for the 2007 superlative (center, T2). Verification: verified, primary source. Note that H.15 and the par yield curve are the same Treasury data and are not two independent sources; the independent support for the superlative is the wire coverage. A Bund clause was cut entirely: a desk gave 3.53 as Friday's close with “highest since April 2011”, but 3.53 is Monday's level, Friday's implies about 3.50, and the April 2011 superlative was pinned to 3.49 and does not survive the move.

3 · Swedish inflation comes in above the Riksbank's own forecast

Statistics Sweden's final August figures put consumer prices at 0.3 percent over twelve months, with CPIF at 0.7 percent against a June forecast of 0.6 and CPIF excluding energy easing to 0.5. Falling food prices pulled the rate down; electricity and fuel pushed it back up.

Statistics Sweden, final August release, 14 September (primary, T1); EFN, Svenska Dagbladet, Dagens industri and Placera for the drivers and the comparison against forecast (center to center-right, T2). Verification: verified, primary source. All four figures match the release. The 7 September flash sits outside the window; today's final release is the hook.

5 · Politics

1 · Sweden's centre-left leads by three seats and the Liberals clear the threshold

The preliminary count gives the opposition 176 of 349 seats against the Tidö parties' 173, roughly 29,000 votes apart. The Liberals took 5.4 percent, clearing four percent after polling at 1.4 percent in January. The Sweden Democrats fell to 17.6 percent from 20.54 in 2022, their first fall since entering parliament. Turnout was about 80 percent.

Valmyndigheten val.se for the rules and the 2022 back series (primary, T1); Sveriges Television Valresultat (center, T1); Associated Press via ABC News (center, T1); Bloomberg (center, T1); The Local Sweden (center, T2). Kristersson has not conceded. Andersson needs both the Left Party, which demands ministerial posts, and the Centre Party, which refuses to sit in a government containing it. Wednesday brings the count of late and overseas votes; the final official result is expected the weekend of 19 to 20 September.

3 · EU sanctions on about 2,600 Russians expire Tuesday without a deal

Ambassadors failed again on 11 September to roll over individual listings covering, on the Council's own count, around 2,600 people and entities, which expire on 15 September. Slovakia demands that Usmanov and Fridman be delisted, and France now backs removing Usmanov. Renewal needs unanimity, so one member state can let them lapse.

Council of the European Union press release for the listing count and the expiry date (primary, T1); Euronews (center, T1); Kyiv Post (center, T2); The New Voice of Ukraine (center, T2); Euromaidan Press (center, T3).
