Top line

The US struck Iran's Qeshm Island and Iranian drones targeted Kuwait for the first time in two months, as Trump landed in Shenzhen for Thursday's summit with Xi and Putin.

1 · The World

1 · US strikes Qeshm; Iran targets Kuwait and Bahrain

On 17 November US jets struck a Revolutionary Guard site on Qeshm and Iran fired at Bahrain again. On 18 November Iranian drones targeted Kuwait's Ali Al Salem base, Iran's first attack on Kuwait in two months; both intercepted them.

BBC (center, T2, “first Iranian attack in two months”); The Washington Post (left, T2); Bloomberg (center, T2); AP via Detroit News and Al-Monitor (center, T2); Al Jazeera (center, T2); Gulf News (center, T2); Fox News (right, T2). Iran's attack on Bahrain was reported before the Qeshm strike, so the order of events is not established; one report disputes that the Qeshm target was a missile site. Iran's claims to have hit Patriot batteries and downed a US MQ-9 drone are unconfirmed and left out. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · Xi greets Trump in Shenzhen; Trump and Putin shake hands

Trump landed in Shenzhen on 18 November and Xi met him on the tarmac; Xi sent an aide to meet Putin. Trump shook hands with Putin on arrival. Trump-Xi talks and a three-way session follow Thursday.

CNN (left, T2); Bloomberg (center, T2); NBC News, ABC News and CBS News (center, T2); BBC and South China Morning Post (center, T2, handshake on arrival); Axios (center, T2); Fox News (right, T2). The aide who met Putin is not named in any checked source. A separate Trump-Putin meeting is confirmed by a US official but not by the Kremlin, so it is left out. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Reuters: Russian refinery runs down about 45 percent

Ukraine says its drones hit the Ilsky refinery in Krasnodar overnight into 18 November. Reuters reported the same day, citing two industry sources, that Russian refinery runs have fallen about 45 percent under the drone campaign.

Reuters via MarketScreener (center, T2, two unnamed industry sources), carried by RBC-Ukraine, Kyiv Independent and UNN; Bloomberg (center, T2, 13 refineries hit since 1 November); The Moscow Times (center, T2). Why it matters here: less Russian refining tightens diesel, Europe's tightest product. The 45 percent rests on one Reuters report and reports differ on the period it covers; a reported hit on the Afipsky refinery is unconfirmed. Verification: single source, unconfirmed.

2 · By region

Europe · Brussels proposes another 45 billion euros for Ukraine

On 18 November the Commission proposed an additional loan of up to 45 billion euros for Ukraine's 2027, 25 billion military and 20 billion budget, from joint EU borrowing, repaid only if Russia pays reparations.

European Commission press release IP/26/2454 (T1, read directly); Reuters explainer via Investing.com (center, T2); Euronews (center, T2); Hungarian Conservative (right, T3). The EU guarantee needs unanimity. Separate from the 45 billion euro tranche proposed on 30 October. Hungary, Slovakia, Czechia and Bulgaria have signalled opt-outs. Verification: verified, primary source.

Middle East · Saudi coalition strikes 60 Houthi targets; pipeline claim

The Saudi-led coalition said it struck 60 Houthi targets as the Houthis pushed toward Aden. The Houthis claimed a drone attack on the East-West pipeline, Saudi Arabia's export route around Hormuz; Riyadh has not confirmed.

Al Jazeera (center, T2); The Washington Post (left, T2); NBC News (center, T2); Washington Times (right, T2); Reuters via US News (center, T2). The pipeline claim is the Houthis' alone and station numbers vary between reports. The Fact-checker could not reach these sources this issue. Verification: could not verify.

Africa · Ethiopia and Eritrea mass troops on their border

Reuters reported on 18 November that both armies have massed within striking distance on the border, after ACLED recorded shelling near Badme. Eritrea's Assab coast sits beside the Bab el-Mandeb shipping war.

Reuters via TimesLive and Al-Monitor (center, T2); ACLED, the conflict monitor (data, T1). Reuters could not independently verify the clashes, and a pro-Ethiopian militia source disputes direct fighting. The Fact-checker could not reach these sources this issue. Verification: could not verify.

3 · Energy

1 · Thursday's SE3 price jumps 27 percent as reactor returns slip

Day-ahead for 19 November: SE3 EUR 192.99 per MWh, from 151.68, and SE4 230.97, from 178.54; SE2 136.44, Germany 224.83. Ringhals 3's return has slipped to 4 December; Ringhals 4 is due 22 November, Forsmark 1 on 27 November.

energy-charts.info, all 96 quarter-hours per zone recomputed independently by two desks (T1); nordiskkarnkraft.se reactor register (T3, restart dates change often). SE4's premium over Germany narrows to 6.14 from 32.29. Verification: verified, primary source.

2 · Oil holds near $92 despite the Gulf attacks

Brent settled at $93.14 on Tuesday, up 70 cents, and traded near $92 at Wednesday's cutoff, as the projected supply surplus outweighed new attacks on Gulf shipping and Gulf states.

Xinhua settlement wire via The Star (center, T2); Kalkine (center, T3, headlines); Reuters (center, T2). Only one feed carries the settlement figure, and Wednesday's is intraday, not a settlement. A reported projectile hit on an ADNOC product tanker on 17 November could not be confirmed by the Fact-checker or a later search, so it is left out. Verification: single source, unconfirmed.

3 · EU agrees faster permits for grids, renewables and storage

Council and Parliament provisionally agreed overnight into 18 November that renewables and grids serve an overriding public interest, with simpler permits for storage, grid connections and charging points. It is the first half of the Grids Package.

Council of the EU press release, 18 Nov 00:39 GMT (T1); Reuters (center, T2); pv magazine and reNEWS (center, T2); Review Energy (center, T2). Still needs formal adoption by both institutions; the transposition deadline was not checked and is left out. Verification: verified, primary source.

4 · Economy

1 · Fed's Waller floats a half-point December hike

Fed Governor Christopher Waller told CNBC on 18 November the Fed “might need to, like, shock people”, backing a half-point rise on 9 December unless it signals a January move. Gold had already hit a 14-month low.

CNBC (center, T2, 12:20 GMT); Reuters via AOL (center, T2); MarketWatch (center, T2); Investor's Business Daily (right, T2). Waller was a leading dove in September; futures fully price a quarter-point December hike. Gold's low came hours before Waller spoke, on hawkish Fed talk, so it is not his doing. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · UK inflation rises to 3.6 percent on record diesel

October CPI rose to 3.6 percent from 3.5, the ONS said on 18 November; motor fuels rose 33.2 percent and diesel hit a record 199.5p a litre. Core inflation held at 2.8 percent and services at 3.7.

ONS bulletin (T1); Reuters (center, T2); The Guardian (left, T2); The Wall Street Journal (right, T2). This is fuel passing through, not broad inflation. Verification: verified, primary source.

3 · Euro area inflation confirmed at 4.0 percent

Eurostat confirmed euro area October inflation at 4.0 percent on 18 November, the highest since 2023, with core revised up. Energy, up about 21 percent, is the main driver, keeping pressure on the ECB to hike again.

Sharecast (center, T3); Anadolu (center, T2); Action Forex and ICIS (center, T2); Investment Week (center, T2, ECB pressure). Eurostat's own release was not reachable to the checker, so the exact core and energy figures and Sweden's harmonised rate are left out. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

5 · Politics

1 · Democrats win Alaska for a 56-seat Senate

AP called Alaska's Senate race for Democrat Mary Peltola on 17 November: she passed 50 percent of first-choice votes against Dan Sullivan, avoiding a ranked-choice count. Democrats hold 56 seats from January.

AP race call via ABC News and NPR (center and left, T2); The New York Times (left, T2); Reuters, CNN, The Washington Post and The Guardian (center and left, T2, 56 to 44). Sullivan has not conceded and Republicans signal challenges. Exact vote counts are left out as unconfirmed. No right-leaning carrier found. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · Netanyahu's opponents take the Knesset speakership

The Knesset elected Matan Kahana of Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar party as speaker on 17 November, 66 to 52, replacing Likud's Amir Ohana. The speaker sets parliament's agenda as Eisenkot tries to form a minority government.

Times of Israel (center, T2); i24 News (center, T2); Bloomberg (center, T2); The Jerusalem Post (right, T2); JTA (left, T2). Eisenkot's minority coalition would rely on the Arab parties Ra'am and the Joint List. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Germany cuts its electricity tax to the EU minimum for 2027

The coalition agreed on 18 November to cut Germany's electricity tax from 2.05 cents per kWh to the EU minimum, 0.1 cent, for everyone in 2027, costing 5.4 billion euros; extension hinges on 2028's budget.

ZDF, MDR and derStandard (center, T2); Bloomberg (center, T2); Handelsblatt and WirtschaftsWoche (right, T2); t-online (center, T2, about 7 percent off a typical household bill). Verification: verified, reliable secondary.
