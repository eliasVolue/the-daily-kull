3 · Energy

1 · Nordic power crisis: Swedish nuclear offline, prices spike twentyfold

SE4 spot hit 200.5 EUR/MWh (week 44), up 66% week-on-week, as Ringhals 3, Ringhals 4, Forsmark 1 remained offline through November, removing 3,300 MW. Hydro reservoirs depleted. Southern Sweden averaged 3 SEK/kWh Nov 9 during cold snap with dead calm.

2 · EU gas storage hits decade low entering winter

EU storage stood at 71.6% on Nov 10, down 10.8 points year-on-year and 17.5 points below five-year average. Germany at 58.0%, lowest for date since 2011. TTF averaged EUR 85.31 in November after spiking to EUR 104 late October.

3 · China coal falls below 50% of power output for first time

Coal generation dropped to 49.7% in H1 2026. Solar capacity surpassed coal by July; renewables generated 37% through Q3. China commissioned 30 GW new coal but retired only 2.7 GW. Historic inflection in world's largest energy consumer.
