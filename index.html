Desk note

Weekend special: this issue covers Friday 16:30 to Monday 16:30. Friday's issue was published late, on Monday morning, with its cutoff kept at Friday 16:30, so Friday evening's market closes and everything since are carried here. Monday's US close came after the cutoff and is left for Tuesday.

Top line

COP31 collapsed without a deal, Wall Street fell into correction then rebounded on Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia, and Ukraine's war reached Libya and the Baltic.

1 · The World

1 · COP31 ends with no deal

COP31 in Antalya ended on 22 November, two days late, with no agreed text, the first such failure since Copenhagen in 2009, Reuters reported. The EU and the Marshall Islands rejected a draft omitting fossil fuels; Saudi Arabia defended it.

UN News, 22 November (T1); Reuters via Express Tribune (center, T2, the only basis for "first since 2009"); Al Jazeera and France 24 with AFP (center, T2); headlines at The Guardian, The New York Times and The Washington Post (left) and The Times and The Telegraph (right). Verification: verified, primary source (outcome); verified, reliable secondary (first since 2009).

2 · Wall Street enters correction, then rebounds on Huang's return

The S&P 500 closed 20 November down 3.7 percent at 6,910.39, more than 10 percent below its October record. On 23 November Nvidia's board reinstated chief executive Jensen Huang; US stocks opened about 4 percent higher and were up about 5 percent by 16:30 CET.

S&P 500 closes from the St. Louis Fed's FRED series (T1); AP closing table via La Nación (center, T2); Nvidia's own release, 23 November (T1); CNBC (center, T2); headlines at the Financial Times (center), The Wall Street Journal (right) and The Guardian (left). Monday's figures are intraday; the close came after the cutoff. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Ukraine's war reaches Libya and the Baltic

Zelensky said on 23 November that Ukrainian naval drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi, Libya, a day earlier. On 21 November a drone strike set the Russian cargo ship Sparta II ablaze off Finland; Kyiv has not claimed it.

Reuters via TBS News (center, T2); Yle, Finland's public broadcaster (center, T2); Al Jazeera (center, T2); NPR (left, T2); Kyiv Independent (center, T2); headline at The Wall Street Journal (right). The sinking rests on Kyiv's account plus satellite imagery; eastern Libya's administration confirmed only an explosion. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · By region

Europe · Poland: three Russian cruise missiles crashed on its soil

Polish prosecutors said on 22 November that three Russian Kh-101 cruise missiles crashed in eastern Poland during the 20 November strike on Ukraine; the army first counted two missiles and a drone. NATO's council met on 23 November.

Anadolu (center, T2, prosecutors); Reuters via US News, headline (center, T2); AP via PBS, 20 November (center, T2); headlines at The Washington Post (left) and The Telegraph and The Times (right). The crashes happened on Friday morning, before the last issue's cutoff, which did not carry them; Tusk called them a deliberate provocation that day. One outlet, Kyiv Post, still reports only one missile confirmed. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

Middle East · Trump floats new Iran strikes next year; Tehran vows to counter blockade

Trump told Fox News on 22 November he may resume strikes on Iran next year if talks fail. Tehran vowed on 23 November to counter the US blockade by all available means. Two tankers were struck off Khasab on 21 November.

Bloomberg via Military.com (center, T2); CNBC (center, T2); Al Jazeera (center, T2); Maritime Executive citing UKMTO and Vanguard (T2); Fox News (right, T2, its own interview). A Houthi claim of a hit on Saudi Arabia's Rabigh refinery on 22 November is unconfirmed. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

North America · Democrats reach 258 House seats, largest majority since 1992

Democrats reached at least 258 of 435 House seats on 22 November when Republican Virginia Foxx lost North Carolina's fifth district after a recount, their largest majority since 1992. It is short of the 290 needed to override a veto.

AP via Kyiv Post (center, T2); headlines at ABC, CBS, NBC, Reuters and BBC (center) and The Guardian and The New York Times (left). The 290 figure is this paper's arithmetic, two thirds of 435. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Energy

1 · Brent slips below 90 dollars despite tanker strikes

Brent settled at 92.57 dollars on 20 November, down 3.4 percent on the week, and traded at 89.86 at 15:02 CET on 23 November, despite two tankers struck off Khasab on Saturday. OPEC+ sets 2027 output on 29 November.

Reuters via Qatar Tribune and Yahoo Finance (center, T2, settlement); OilPrice (T2, Monday's intraday quote); Maritime Executive citing UKMTO (T2); headline at The Wall Street Journal (right). Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · TTF jumps on cold forecast, then gives back most

Front-month TTF rose 8.6 percent to 96.08 euros per MWh early on 23 November as forecasts turned colder, then gave back most of the gain, trading near 91 later in the day. Both are intraday quotes.

OilPrice citing Bloomberg, 09:30 CET (center, T2); Trading Economics (T3, later quote, no clock time). The Fact-checker's own market data read gives the same 8.6 percent and 91.31 euros at 12:54 CET. Verification: single source, unconfirmed (each level).

3 · SE3 day-ahead doubles to 191 euros for Tuesday

Tuesday's day-ahead prices average 191.40 euros per MWh in SE3, up from 141.11 for Monday and 93.32 for Saturday; SE4 249.11, NO1 232.87, Germany 254.15, SE2 47.91. Ringhals 4's restart slipped from Sunday to Monday evening.

energy-charts.info day-ahead, daily average of all 96 quarter-hours, recomputed by the desk and again by the Fact-checker (T1); SVT Halland, 22 November (center, T2, Ringhals 4). SE3 doubles against Saturday; against Monday it is up 36 percent. Verification: verified, primary source.

4 · Economy

1 · S&P 500 gives up nearly all of 2026's gain

The S&P 500's seventh straight loss on 20 November left it up 0.9 percent for 2026; the Nasdaq fell 5.3 percent to 22,617.73, down 10 percent on the week. Nvidia rose more than 30 percent on 23 November after Huang's reinstatement.

FRED S&P 500 series (T1, seven straight lower closes from 12 to 20 November); AP closing table via La Nación (center, T2); CNBC (center, T2); headline at The Wall Street Journal (right). Nvidia's move is an intraday quote. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · Ten year Treasury yield back below 5 percent

The ten year Treasury yield closed 20 November at 4.92 percent, down from 5.04 a day earlier and 5.15 a week before, as investors fled stocks. Futures still put odds of a Fed rise on 9 December above 90 percent.

US Treasury daily par yield curve (T1); CNBC, 23 November (center, T2, hike odds). Verification: verified, primary source (yields); single source, unconfirmed (hike odds).

3 · Euro area growth steady as Germany surges and France shrinks

The euro area's flash composite PMI held at 51.7 in November, above the 50 line that separates growth from contraction. Germany's rose to 55.0 from 53.4; France's fell to 46.2, a six-month low, with manufacturing at 47.9.

Reuters via RTÉ, 23 November (center, T2, euro area figures and France's six-month low); HCOB flash tables as carried by investingLive (T3 carrier of primary data, Germany and France). Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

5 · Politics

1 · COP31: who blocked what

Summit president Murat Kurum declared no agreement after the EU, Panama and the Marshall Islands, among others, objected to a cover text omitting fossil fuels. UN climate chief Simon Stiell pointed to an agreement on a just transition as progress. COP32 is in Addis Ababa.

UN News, 22 November, read in full (T1); headlines at the Financial Times (center), The Times (right), The Guardian (left), Svenska Dagbladet (right) and Dagens Nyheter (left). Verification: verified, primary source.

2 · Sweden: Left Party rejects Andersson's new offer

Magdalena Andersson on 22 November offered the Left Party a seat in an inner cabinet but no ministers; deputy leader Ida Gabrielsson refused the same day. The Speaker's next round is Tuesday, ten weeks after the election.

SVT Nyheter, 22 November, two articles (center, T2); Riksdag press release via Omni (T1, Speaker's talks 24 November); Svenska Dagbladet (right, T2); headline at Dagens Nyheter (left). The offer came jointly from the Social Democrats, Centre and Greens; Svenska Dagbladet reports it is a floor for further talks, not a last bid. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Spain's final polls point to a right-wing majority

Final polls published by 23 November put the People's Party and Vox together above the 176 seats needed for a majority in all but one. 40dB for El País: People's Party 31.8 percent, Socialists 30.9, Vox 17.3. Spain votes 29 November.

Reuters via The Star, 23 November (center, T2); headlines at El País (left), La Vanguardia (center) and ABC (right). The state pollster CIS is the outlier, with the Socialists ahead on 34.4. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.
