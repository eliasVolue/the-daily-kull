Top line

Houthis took the Bab el-Mandeb coast while Hormuz stays contested, putting both exits for Gulf oil under Iran-aligned pressure; Trump, Xi and Putin meet Thursday.

1 · The World

1 · Houthis take the Bab el-Mandeb coast and advance on Aden

On 15 November Yemen's government-aligned forces withdrew from Dhubab and the Bab el-Mandeb area to defend Aden; the Houthis now hold the western coast, about 80 km from Aden. Saudi Arabia replaced its coalition commander on 16 November.

Reuters via US News (center, T2); France 24/AFP (center, T2); Al Jazeera (center, T2); NBC News (left, T2); Jerusalem Post (right, T2). Confidence: multiple independent sources. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · Trump, Xi and Putin confirm first three-way summit, Thursday

China's foreign ministry and the White House schedule confirmed on 16 November a Trump, Xi and Putin meeting on 19 November in Shenzhen, beside APEC. Nuclear arms lead the agenda, with Iran and trade; New START, the last US-Russia arms treaty, expired in February.

CNBC (center, T2); NBC News (left, T2); PBS (center, T2); Al Jazeera (center, T2); TASS (state, T2). Confidence: multiple independent sources. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Zelensky says US floated Donbas for NATO membership

Zelensky said in remarks reported 14 November that US envoys asked, for the first time, whether Kyiv would leave the Donbas it still holds in exchange for NATO membership. He refused to cede territory but offered a referendum on a full security package.

Kyiv Independent (center, T2); CNBC (center, T2); France 24 (center, T2); NBC News (left, T2); Jerusalem Post (right, T2). Confidence: all reports rest on Zelensky's own account; no US confirmation. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · By region

Europe · Estonia moves to close its Russian border after drone plant sabotage

Prime Minister Kristen Michal said on 15 November the Narva, Luhamaa and Koidula crossings should close from 1 December, after a foiled arson attempt attributed to Russian military intelligence on drone maker Threod Systems near Tallinn on 14 November. Government decides next week.

ERR News (center, T1); CNBC (center, T2); Euronews (center, T2); Defense News (center, T2); Moscow Times (center, T2). Confidence: multiple independent sources; a proposal, not yet a decision. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

North America · US requires licences for AI chips to five more countries

On 16 November the Commerce Department required licences to ship advanced AI chips to Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil, and for data centres there to rent that compute to Chinese firms. Nvidia was down about 4 percent intraday.

Bloomberg (center, T2); CNBC (center, T2); Al Jazeera (center, T2); OPB/NPR (left, T2); Forbes (right, T2). Confidence: multiple independent sources. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

Africa · Africa CDC: Congo Ebola “still not under control”

Six months in, Africa CDC reported on 15 November over 11,200 cases and about 5,500 deaths, with over 80 percent of new cases arising outside contact lists. Only 1.3 billion of 2.9 billion dollars pledged has been released. No vaccine is approved for this strain.

Reuters via Daily Maverick and Khaleej Times (center, T2); Al Jazeera (center, T2); ECDC situation update (T1). Confidence: multiple independent sources. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Energy

1 · Iran strikes Bahrain again and claims six tanker mine hits

Iran launched six drones at Bahrain's Riffa air base on 15 November, its second strike there in three days and the first run since September. The Revolutionary Guards claim six tankers have hit Hormuz mines since 13 November; only one fire is independently confirmed.

Al Jazeera (center, T2); CNBC (center, T2); Euronews (center, T2); Reuters via Investing.com (center, T2). Confidence: multiple sources on the Bahrain strikes; the tanker count is an IRGC claim with inconsistent totals. Verification: single source, unconfirmed (tanker count).

2 · Oil and gas ease despite the attacks

Brent settled at $95.84 on Friday, a second weekly loss, opened near $95 Monday and fell below $94 by the European afternoon as Gulf crude flowed near pre-war levels. TTF ended Friday near EUR 91 per MWh and slipped below 90 Monday; EU storage is about 71 percent.

Newsquawk (center, T2); CNBC carrying Reuters (center, T2); Trading Economics (center, T2). Post-cutoff: Brent settled $92.44 Monday evening. TTF levels are vendor end-of-day, not exchange settlements. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Swedish power doubles in SE3 for Tuesday

Day-ahead for Tuesday 17 November, auctioned Monday, averaged EUR 205.10 per MWh in SE3 and 227.14 in SE4, against 95.57 and 146.92 for Friday delivery. Germany rose to 211.58 from 161.73. The narrow SE3 to SE4 spread points to system-wide tightness; no cause confirmed.

energy-charts.info, all 96 quarter-hours recomputed independently by two desks (T1). Cause unverified. Verification: verified, primary source.

4 · Economy

1 · Japan's economy shrinks in the third quarter

Japan's Cabinet Office said on 16 November real GDP fell 0.1 percent annualised in July to September, against 0.3 percent growth expected, with recovering energy imports a drag. The Bank of Japan is still expected to weigh a third hike this year in December.

Cabinet Office ESRI (T1); The Star carrying Reuters (center, T2); CNBC (center, T2); Nikkei Asia (center, T2). Confidence: multiple independent sources on the headline figure. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · China's October data misses across the board

China's statistics bureau said on 16 November October industrial output rose 4.8 percent, against 5.3 in a Reuters poll, and retail sales just 0.4 percent. Fixed investment fell 8.1 percent and property investment 21.6 percent, January to October.

National Bureau of Statistics release (T1); Business Standard carrying Reuters (center, T2); Quartz (center, T2). Verification: verified, primary source.

3 · European rate setters talk of hikes into 2027

ECB Governing Council member Šimkus called a December hike his central scenario on 16 November and said more tightening next year may be needed; the Bank of England's Pill said on 14 November hikes may run into 2027. Euro area industry was 1.5 percent below a year ago in September.

Newsquawk citing Econostream (center, T2); Financial Times interview via Investing.com and The Guardian (left, T2); Eurostat (T1). Verification: single source, unconfirmed (Šimkus).

5 · Politics

1 · Dutch coalition spends the weekend in crisis over wealth tax

Prime Minister Jetten's minority cabinet of D66, CDA and VVD held crisis talks 13 to 15 November after the VVD voted on 12 November against its own 2027 tax plan, passed with opposition votes for a higher wealth tax. Unresolved at the cutoff.

NL Times carrying ANP (center, T2); DutchNews (center, T2); Euronews (center, T2); The National (center, T2). Post-cutoff and unverified: the parties said Monday night confidence was restored. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · Netanyahu opens unity talks with Eisenkot

Likud and Eisenkot's party held unity talks on 15 November. Eisenkot has held the mandate to form a government since 8 November; neither bloc reached 61 of 120 seats in the 27 October election. Failure means another election in early 2027.

Times of Israel (center, T2); Israel Hayom (right, T2); JNS (right, T2); Al Jazeera (center, T2). Bennett's participation reported by one desk source only, so left out. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Republicans start the 2028 fight after the midterm rout

With Democrats holding about 256 House seats to 169 on the latest count, Reuters reported on 16 November that Vance is no longer seen as Trump's inevitable successor. A Democratic Congress sits from 3 January, narrowing Trump's room on Iran, tariffs and Ukraine.

Reuters via US News (center, T2); Cook Political Report (center, T2, page blocked to the checker); McClatchy (center, T2). Seat count and Reuters line not independently confirmed. Verification: could not verify.
