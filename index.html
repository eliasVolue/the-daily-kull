1 · The World

1 · The Federal Reserve raises rates, its first increase since 2023

The Federal Open Market Committee voted 12-0 to lift the target range a quarter point to 3.75 to 4.00 percent at 18:00 GMT Wednesday. Chair Kevin Warsh told the press conference, “The plain fact is that inflation is too high and has been for too long.” Sixteen of eighteen officials expect at least one further rise this year, on a median of 4.1 percent.

federalreserve.gov statement and Summary of Economic Projections (primary, T1, 18:00 GMT Wednesday); Bloomberg (center wire, T1); Washington Post (left, T1); Fox Business (right, T2, 20:01 GMT); CNBC (center, T1). Verification: verified, primary source. One repair: a desk carried the chair's words as the bare fragment “too high for too long” inside quotation marks, which is not the sentence he spoke, so the full quote now stands in its place. The statement's own language is that inflation remains elevated and that the action supports a timelier return to the 2 percent goal. The dot plot resolves as twelve of eighteen officials seeing one further rise and four seeing two, hence sixteen seeing at least one, on a median of 4.125 percent.

3 · A UN mission finds US war crimes in Iran and Iranian crimes against humanity

A Human Rights Council fact-finding mission found reasonable grounds that a US strike in February on the Shajareh Tayyebeh school in Minab killed more than 150 people, about 120 of them children, and that a separate strike on a Lamerd sports centre killed 22 more civilians. It also found Iran's post-war crackdown amounted to crimes against humanity, including at least 29 executions.

Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights release, read through carriers because ohchr.org returns 403 to this desk (primary finding, T1); NBC News (center, T1); the National (center, T2); NPR (leans left, T1). Verification: verified, reliable secondary. One repair of substance: a desk applied the 150 dead and 120 children to both strike sites combined. That figure belongs to the Minab school alone, and the Lamerd sports centre is a separate 22 civilians. This is the same failure family as the combined-toll error this paper logged in early September, now recurring on a United Nations finding rather than a disaster toll.

3 · Euro area inflation confirmed at 3.2 percent, revised down from the flash

Eurostat's final August reading put euro area annual inflation at 3.2 percent, below the 3.3 flash and up from 2.9 percent in July. All-items excluding energy was 2.1 percent and services 3.0. Services is the largest contributor at 1.43 points, energy close behind at 1.29.

Eurostat news release 2-17092026-ap, fetched directly including its contributions table (primary, T1). Verification: verified, primary source. One repair: a desk wrote that energy remains the largest contributor. Andersson still needs both the Left Party, which demands ministries, and the Centre Party, which refuses to sit with it.
