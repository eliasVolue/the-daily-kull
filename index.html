Top line

A strike killed a worker and briefly halted some operations at Saudi Arabia's Jazan refinery; EU leaders left without a budget, and Centre Party leaders back Kristersson before Sunday's party council.

1 · The World

1 · Strike partly halts Saudi Arabia's Jazan refinery

A projectile hit Aramco's 400,000 barrel a day Jazan refinery on Thursday, killing a Nepali worker, injuring several and temporarily halting some operations, Saudi Arabia's energy ministry said. The coalition blamed the Houthis. No restart date or output loss was given.

Reuters via NBC News (center wire, T2); Al Jazeera (center, T2); Bloomberg via Rigzone (center, T2); Khaleej Times (center, T2). All trace to the ministry statement. The Houthis claimed attacks on Ras Tanura and the Jeddah and Jazan ports a day earlier; unverified. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · Hormuz traffic at 54 percent of pre-war

Kpler says total Hormuz clearances averaged 9.25 million barrels a day in the seven days to 24 November, about 54 percent of the pre-war 17.14 million. Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed late Thursday that a tanker on an "unauthorized" route hit mines; nobody has confirmed it.

The National citing Kpler (center, T2); CBS News citing IRIB (center, T2); Xinhua (center, T2); Jerusalem Post (right, T2); Iran International (right, T3, no UKMTO confirmation). No vessel, flag or casualties named. The figure is Hormuz only; Gulf exports via bypass routes are not counted. Verification: verified, reliable secondary (flows); single source, unconfirmed (the mine claim).

3 · Beijing calls in state funds; Chinese stocks jump

The State Council called for a bigger role for the state-backed "national team" funds, led by Central Huijin, to steady markets. The CSI 300 rose about 4 percent, its biggest gain since October 2024, and the chip index a record 17.6 percent.

CNBC (center, T2); Reuters via Yahoo Finance (center, T2); Finimize (T3). Outlets differ on which mainland index rose 4.2 and which 3.7. Beijing is now pledging state money to stop the AI and chip sell-off. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · By region

Europe EU budget summit collapses; talks resume 17 December

The summit ended just after midnight with no deal on the 2028 to 2034 budget, and day two was cancelled. Seven frugal states including Sweden want deeper cuts; Slovakia, Hungary and Czechia still oppose the 45 billion euro Ukraine loan.

Euronews (center, T2); AP via The Washington Post (left, T2); Eunews (center, T2); bne IntelliNews (center, T2). Macron threatened to end negotiations after 31 December. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

Asia and Pacific Taiwan votes Saturday after Chinese air patrol

Taiwan elects mayors and magistrates in all 22 cities and counties on 28 November. On the last campaign day 30 Chinese aircraft were detected around the island, 26 across the median line. Beijing has denounced Puma Shen, the ruling party's Taipei mayoral candidate, who is wanted by Chinese police for "separatism".

Reuters (center, T2); Nikkei Asia (center, T2); Focus Taiwan (center, T2). Preview; results Saturday evening, analysis Monday. The vote is seen as a test before the 2028 presidential election. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

Africa Generic HIV prevention jab reaches Africa at 40 dollars

The first generic lenacapavir, made by Hetero, arrived in Zambia on Wednesday, with Uganda next and Zimbabwe later. It costs 40 dollars per person a year in 120 licensed countries, against about 28,000 for the brand in the US.

Euronews (center, T2); Semafor (center, T2); Pharmaphorum (center, T2); Global Fund announcement, 25 November (T1, not read directly). Arrived just before the window, not covered before. A twice yearly jab at this price can bend the HIV curve; most people with HIV live in sub-Saharan Africa. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Energy

1 · Saturday: Nordic prices fall by a third to a half

Saturday's day-ahead averages: SE3 154.80 euros per MWh (Friday 276.60), SE4 186.64 (315.89), Germany 191.77 (301.82), NO1 187.10 (272.43), FI 99.50 (200.68), SE2 65.88 (134.63). SE4 is back below Germany.

energy-charts.info day-ahead, daily average of all 96 quarter-hours for 28 November delivery, recomputed by the desk (T1; Finland on the central European delivery day). SE1 68.19. The cause, wind or weekend demand, is unverified. Verification: verified, primary source.

2 · OPEC+ set to freeze quotas; January Brent near 96

OPEC+ has agreed in principle to hold output targets unchanged in the first quarter of 2027 at Sunday's meeting, three sources told Reuters. January Brent settled at 96.57 dollars Thursday, up 1.96, and traded at 95.38 at 16:38 CET Friday, intraday.

Reuters via Yahoo Finance and RTE (center, T2 carriers); CNBC quote service (center, T2, timed quotes). A freeze defers the Iraq and Kazakhstan capacity fight. Reuters also reports the US urged Venezuela and Iraq to consider leaving OPEC. January expires 30 November. Verification: verified, reliable secondary (prices); single source, unconfirmed (the OPEC+ agreement in principle).

3 · Olkiluoto 3 held at 440 MW until Monday

Olkiluoto 3, 1,600 MW, has been limited to about 440 MW for a fault investigation since 15:00 CET Thursday. TVO has extended the limit twice, now to 01:00 Finnish time on 30 November, with a test near 100 MW on Saturday.

Teollisuuden Voima market notices via the Nord Pool UMM API, versions 1 to 3, 26 November 14:58 to 27 November 16:40 CET (T1). Forsmark 2 stays out until 2 December. Verification: verified, primary source.

4 · Economy

1 · French and Spanish inflation fall as fuel eases

France's harmonised inflation fell to 3.2 percent in November from 3.7, the national index to 2.9 from 3.4. Spain's fell to 4.1 from 4.9, core to 2.6 from 3.1. Both are the first large euro area readings before the ECB's 17 December decision.

INE flash estimate, 27 November (T1); INSEE provisional estimate, 27 November (T1); Euronews (center, T2); Reuters (center, T2). They weaken the case for the rise the ECB's October account set out. Verification: verified, primary source.

2 · Sweden grows 0.5 percent but households turn gloomy

Statistics Sweden's full release put third quarter growth at 0.5 percent on the quarter, 2.8 on the year, led by household consumption. The same day, Konjunkturinstitutet's household confidence fell to 90.3 from 94.2, the overall indicator to 104.7 from 107.4.

Statistics Sweden, 27 November (T1); Konjunkturinstitutet, 27 November (T1, headline); Göteborgs-Posten (right, T2); Börsvärlden (center, T3). Net exports were a drag. Verification: verified, primary source.

3 · Euro area sentiment falls again

The European Commission's economic sentiment indicator fell further to 95.8 in November from 96.5, below the 97.2 expected and the lowest since June. Industry fell to minus 5.1; selling price expectations reached a six month high.

European Commission business and consumer survey (T1, headline); Anadolu Agency (center, T2); Il Sole 24 Ore (right, T2); Brussels Signal citing a Reuters poll (right, T3); Trading Economics (T3). Weaker activity with higher price plans is the mix the ECB faces in December. Verification: verified, reliable secondary (95.8 from 96.5); single source, unconfirmed (consensus, industry and price detail).

5 · Politics

1 · Sweden: Centre leaders back Kristersson; council decides Sunday

Centre leader Thand Ringqvist said her board and MPs back a yes on Monday and joining a four party minority government; at least a third voted no, SVT reports. The party's trust council decides Sunday. Kristersson falls only if 175 MPs vote against him.

SVT Nyheter (center, T2); TV4 (center, T2); Fokus (right, T2); Riksdagen (T1, seats); Brussels Signal (right, T3, the Sweden Democrats' stance). The left bloc holds 151 seats; with the Sweden Democrats reportedly abstaining, only an active no from at least 24 of Centre's 25 MPs blocks him. Their abstention is not yet formally decided. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

2 · Israel: Eisenkot skips extension; Bennett prefers unity with Likud

Gadi Eisenkot said he aims to form a government within the eight days left on his mandate and will not seek an extension. Naftali Bennett said he prefers a unity government with Likud under Eisenkot as prime minister.

Times of Israel (center, T2); Jerusalem Post via Yahoo (right, T2). Bennett has hedged before; the bloc targets signed deals by 1 to 2 December. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Zelensky likens Trump's Putin friendship to befriending Hitler

Zelensky told the BBC on Thursday that Trump calling Putin his friend is like calling Hitler a friend. Overnight Russia launched 155 drones at Ukraine, 110 shot down, causing power outages in six regions.

BBC via ABC Australia (center, T2); Reuters (center, T2); Ukrinform (center, T2); Kyiv Independent (center, T2); The Daily Beast (left, T3). Earlier this week Kushner said Putin "has agreed not to use nuclear weapons" (Meduza). No White House response by the cutoff. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.
