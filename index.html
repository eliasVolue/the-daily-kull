Top line

The Gulf war widened to Saudi Arabia's Red Sea ports and killed two Italian workers in Kuwait; January Brent passed 95 dollars, and the euro area picked its next central bank chief.

1 · The World

1 · Houthis strike Saudi Arabia's Red Sea ports

On Wednesday evening the Houthis claimed strikes on Jeddah and Jizan ports. The coalition says a projectile caused minor damage at Jizan, a drone was downed near Jeddah and two missiles aimed at Riyadh were intercepted.

Al Jazeera (center, T2); The National (center, T2); Bloomberg (center, T2); The Wall Street Journal (right, T2, oil market reaction); The Guardian (left, T2, context). Saudi Arabia's Red Sea coast, chiefly Yanbu, is its export route around Hormuz. Any damage at Jeddah is the Houthis' claim only. Verification: verified, reliable secondary (strikes and interceptions); damage at Jeddah unconfirmed.

2 · China threatens response to US tariffs; EU defends its cap

China's commerce ministry said Beijing "firmly opposes" the proposed Section 301 tariffs and reserves the right to respond, naming no measures. The European Commission rejected the US case and expects its 15 percent cap to hold.

Xinhua (T1 for China's position); The Guardian (left, T2); France 24 (center, T2); Politico Europe (center, T2, Commission spokesperson Olof Gill); Bloomberg (center, T2). A 40 percent figure for China is circulating; the USTR text says up to 20 percent extra. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

AP via ABC News (center, T2); Yahoo Finance (center, T2); FRED NASDAQCOM (T1); headline at The New York Times (left); Investing.com via Yahoo Finance (center, T2, the rating action; S&P's own release not reached). 2 · By region

Middle East Iranian drones kill two Italian workers in Kuwait

Two Rheinmetall Italia technicians died when Iranian drones hit a Kuwaiti site where they serviced air defence radars on Thursday; a Kuwaiti and a Pakistani were wounded. Kuwait's defence ministry and Italy's foreign minister Tajani confirmed the deaths.

CNN (left, T2); Bloomberg (center, T2); ABC News (center, T2); Anadolu (center, T2). Tehran gave no immediate comment. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

Europe EU budget summit opens divided; deal targeted for December

Leaders split over Costa's 1,582 billion euro budget for 2028 to 2034. Slovakia, Czechia, Hungary and Bulgaria object to a 45 billion euro Ukraine loan for 2027; Merz said "there is a link" to the budget. The target is agreement on 17 to 18 December.

Reuters (center, T2); AP via Korea Times (center, T2); Euronews (center, T2); RTE (center, T2). Merz's threat to block the budget was reported on 18 November. Sweden is a net contributor pushing for a smaller budget. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

Africa Congo's Ebola outbreak passes 12,000 cases

ECDC counts 12,021 confirmed Bundibugyo Ebola cases and 5,904 deaths in Congo to 24 November, across eight provinces, up from WHO's 11,613 on 17 November. This strain has no licensed vaccine.

ECDC update, 26 November (T1); WHO Disease Outbreak News, 20 November (T1). Kenya and Nigeria are counting down 42 days to declare their outbreaks over. Verification: verified, primary source.

2 · Forsmark 1 returns as Forsmark 2 stops

Forsmark 1's second turbine reconnected at 06:30 Thursday, with normal operation expected around midday Friday. Forsmark 2, 1,121 MW, goes to cold shutdown from 16:00 Friday to 08:00 on 2 December; Ringhals 3 stays out until 20 December.

Forsmarks Kraftgrupp market notices via Nord Pool, 26 November 05:41 and 30 October (T1); Vattenfall (T1, background). Verification: verified, primary source.

3 · January Brent passes 95 dollars before OPEC+ meets

January Brent settled at 94.61 dollars on 25 November, up 3.21, and traded at 95.58 at 14:08 CET Thursday, intraday, within a 93.48 to 96.20 range; February traded near 91.5. Kazakhstan has joined Iraq in disputing its capacity rating before OPEC+ meets Sunday.

Reuters via Kotak Neo (center, T2 carrier, settlement), reproduced by Yahoo Finance; CNBC quote service (center, T2, intraday, rechecked by the Fact-checker); OilPrice.com citing Reuters (center, T2); Reuters via piqmarkets (Kazakhstan). The January contract expires 30 November. Iran's Revolutionary Guards also claim a gas carrier hit a mine near Hormuz; Reuters could not verify it. Verification: verified, reliable secondary (prices); single source, unconfirmed (the mine claim).

4 · Economy

1 · ECB account points to a December rise

The account of the ECB's 28 to 29 October meeting says "a large number of members" could have backed a rise then; another "would likely be necessary" unless inflation improves significantly. The deposit rate is 2.50 percent; the next decision is 17 December.

ECB account, 26 November (T1); Reuters via Global Banking and Finance (center, T2); FXStreet (T3). Verification: verified, primary source.

2 · Bank of Korea raises its rate to 3.25 percent

The Bank of Korea raised its rate a quarter point to 3.25 percent on Thursday, its third rise this year, by a 5 to 2 vote. It lifted its 2027 growth forecast to 4.1 percent from 2.9 on a chip export boom.

Bank of Korea press release, 26 November (T1); CNBC (center, T2); The Star carrying Reuters and Bloomberg (center, T2). Korea Herald (center, T2) names the two dissenters. Verification: verified, primary source.

3 · Norway's economy shrinks 0.3 percent in the third quarter

Statistics Norway: total GDP fell 0.3 percent on the quarter; mainland GDP, excluding oil and gas, grew 0.1 percent against 0.3 expected. Oil and gas investment fell 8.5 percent. Year on year, total growth slowed to 0.9 percent from 2.7.

Statistics Norway national accounts, 26 November (T1); Reuters via SquawkNews (center, T2). Sweden's third quarter GDP is due Friday at 08:00. Verification: verified, primary source.

5 · Politics

1 · Sweden: Centre Party weighs backing Kristersson

The Centre Party's board and MPs met in emergency session on Thursday; sources told TV4 there is support for a yes on Monday. Rebels are drafting a no motion; the party council decides, probably at the weekend. The Sweden Democrats will abstain.

SVT Nyheter (center, T2); TV4 (center, T2), carrying Dagens Nyheter (left, T2) on the counter motion; The Local (center, T3). All rest on unnamed party sources; no outcome was reported by 16:15. With the Sweden Democrats abstaining, a Centre yes elects Kristersson. Verification: verified, reliable secondary (from unnamed sources).

2 · Spain's Hernández de Cos picked to lead the ECB

A majority of euro area leaders backed Pablo Hernández de Cos, head of the Bank for International Settlements and former Bank of Spain governor, to succeed Christine Lagarde, who leaves on 7 February 2027, after the Netherlands dropped Klaas Knot.

Financial Times (center, T2); Reuters (center, T2); CNBC (center, T2); Euronews (center, T2); Il Sole 24 Ore (right, T2). Not yet formal: a European Parliament hearing is reportedly set for 7 December before the European Council appoints him. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.

3 · Israel: Eisenkot adds the Democrats, reaching 36 seats

Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar agreed to sign a coalition deal with Yair Golan's Democrats on Thursday, two days after they froze talks, promising repeal of the judicial overhaul. With Liberman's party the bloc holds 36 of the 61 seats needed.

Reuters (center, T2); Times of Israel (center, T2); Jerusalem Post (right, T2); ABC Australia (center, T2). Talks with United Torah Judaism continue; his mandate runs to 6 December, extendable to 20 December. Verification: verified, reliable secondary.
